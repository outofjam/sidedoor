# Privacy policy

*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

Sidedoor for X Links does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

**What the extension does locally**

- It reads link addresses on the pages you visit, only to find links to x.com or twitter.com and point them at the viewer you chose. Nothing about the pages or links leaves your browser.
- It watches page loads to x.com and twitter.com, only to redirect them to your viewer.
- It saves your settings (on/off switches, chosen viewer, any viewers you add) in Firefox's local extension storage on your device.

**What it doesn't do**

- No analytics, telemetry, tracking or crash reporting.
- No requests to any server run by the developer. There isn't one.
- No access to your X account, cookies or passwords.

**Third-party viewer sites**

When you open a link, your browser visits the viewer site you selected, as it would any other website. That site sees your visit and its own privacy policy applies. Sidedoor isn't affiliated with any viewer site.

**Permissions, and why**

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to x.com and twitter.com | Redirect those pages to your viewer |
| Read links on all websites | Rewrite X links wherever they appear |
| webRequest / webRequestBlocking | Redirect x.com page loads before they happen |
| menus | The right-click "Open original on X" and "Open with" options |
| tabs | Open those right-click choices in a new tab next to the current one |
| storage | Save your settings |

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/outofjam/sidedoor/issues.
