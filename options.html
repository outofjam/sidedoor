Sidedoor for X Links

Opens x.com and twitter.com links in a viewer of your choice.

General

Viewer

Pick where X links should open. If one stops working, switch to another or add your own.

Add a viewer

{path} is everything after x.com/ (for example NASA/status/123), {query} is the query string, and {url} is the full x.com link, encoded, for sites that take ?url=….

Privacy

Sidedoor doesn't collect, store or send any data. Everything happens in your browser. When you open a link, you're visiting the viewer site you chose, and that site's own privacy policy applies. Sidedoor isn't affiliated with X Corp. or any viewer site.
